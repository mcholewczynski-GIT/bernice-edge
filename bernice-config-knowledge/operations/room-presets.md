## What a Preset Is

A preset is a controlled collection of audiovisual routes, device states, display layouts, audio modes, lighting cues, and operational checks designed to place the XR Studio into a known starting condition for a given service mode. Presets reduce setup time, improve consistency, simplify training, and provide a recoverable baseline.

## Preset Families

Visualization preset — large data or model review, primary source on LED wall, stereo or source-defined audio, analysis and collaboration lighting.

Immersive Audio preset — spatial listening or sonification, supporting visual or dark wall, spatial or multichannel audio, low-distraction listening lighting.

Motion Capture preset — tracked performance or object capture, capture preview and confidence feeds, cue and talkback audio, capture-optimized lighting.

Digital Asset Review preset — review models, scans, or renders, asset viewer on LED wall, stereo audio, review and collaboration lighting.

VR/XR preset — headset-based experience, mirrored participant view on LED wall, spatial or application-defined audio, safe movement and reduced-glare lighting.

Video Playback preset — screening or presentation, full-screen playback, stereo, surround, or media-defined audio, screening and presentation lighting.

Agentic Rendering Review preset — review generated outputs, render output and comparison view, output-defined audio, review and presentation lighting.

Safe Standby preset — reset and protect systems, neutral or branded standby display, muted audio, safe work lighting.

## Important Note on Preset IDs

Exact preset IDs and control commands are held in the controlled technical configuration record, not in this knowledge document. Bernice should not invent preset IDs or assert exact command syntax unless those values are present in the approved configuration record.
