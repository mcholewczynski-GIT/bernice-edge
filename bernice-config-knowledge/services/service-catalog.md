## Available Service Modes

The XR Studio supports eight service modes.

### 1. Large-Scale Visualization
Uses the XR Studio LED video wall to display complex data, high-resolution imagery, simulations, models, maps, timelines, and other visual materials at a scale that supports group analysis and shared understanding. Typical uses: scientific visualization, geospatial data review, architectural exploration, research presentations, interactive dashboards.

### 2. Immersive and Spatial Audio
Uses the XR Studio total-surround and spatial audio system to create a sound field extending beyond conventional stereo. Supports research, creative work, simulation, sonification, spatial media, and experiential presentations. Typical uses: acoustic research, data sonification, immersive film playback, environmental soundscapes, multichannel composition.

### 3. Motion Capture and Positional Tracking
Allows users to record, analyze, and apply human or object movement within a tracked volume. Supports research, animation, biomechanics, performance, virtual production, and digital asset creation. Calibration always requires a technician.

### 4. Digital Asset Creation and Digitization
Converts physical, conceptual, or existing media into digital objects that can be viewed, analyzed, rendered, or integrated into immersive environments. Typical uses: 3D modeling, photogrammetry, digital-twin preparation, asset optimization for real-time engines.

### 5. Virtual Reality and Extended Reality (VR/XR)
Enables users to enter, test, review, or collaborate within virtual and extended environments using head-mounted displays, tracked movement, spatial audio, and large-scale visualization. Observers can view a mirrored experience on the LED wall.

### 6. General Video and Media Playback
Straightforward mode for presentations, films, demonstrations, web content, remote participants, and conventional audiovisual uses. Typical uses: video playback, presentations, review of rendered media, hybrid collaboration.

### 7. Technician-Assisted Service
Supports activities requiring advanced setup, calibration, live operation, custom routing, safety review, research integration, or troubleshooting beyond Bernice approved autonomous actions. Required for: custom workflows, live production, spatial audio design, complex multi-system integrations.

### 8. Agentic Rendering and Generative Media
Helps authorized users transform approved research data, source material, prompts, and project assets into visual, auditory, or multimodal outputs that can be experienced within the XR Studio. Requires data authorization confirmation before any job begins.

## Intent-to-Service Quick Reference

What the user wants to do / Recommended service:
- Show a dataset to the whole group: Large-Scale Visualization
- Hear how data changes around the room: Immersive and Spatial Audio
- Capture a person movement: Motion Capture and Positional Tracking
- Create a 3D model of an object: Digital Asset Creation
- Let participants enter a virtual environment: Virtual Reality and Extended Reality
- Play a film or presentation: General Video and Media Playback
- Need a custom setup or live operator: Technician-Assisted Service
- Generate a visual or audio experience from project files: Agentic Rendering and Generative Media
